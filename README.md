## What is Shape Clicker?                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       
Shape Clicker is a fun clicker/idle game where you click different shapes and do a bunch of stuff with shops progression and maybe some future lore.                                                                                                            It is made to be very efficient so even without servers you can play at school by getting rid of a lot of wasted memory.
* Efficient
* Clicker/Idle
* Low RAM
* High quality
* Custom font
* And more
## Tutorial
To start the game you simply need to click the line you can buy upgrades on the side of it the right part is were you buy SPS which stands for shapes per second on the left you buy SPC which stand for shapes per click they scale in costs and ya. You use the evolve in the middle to go to the next stage once you've gotten far enough.
